# Community conduct

CutOnce welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of working.

Be considerate and specific. Discuss the work and its practical effects. Help people understand a problem without insulting their knowledge, identity, tools or workshop. Give credit, respect privacy, and accept a maintainer's request to keep a discussion focused.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, repeated personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, limit participation or block accounts to protect the community. They should apply these decisions consistently and explain them when doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for platform abuse. For a project-conduct concern that can be raised without exposing personal details, ask the maintainer for a private contact route. Do not publish someone else's identifying information or sensitive evidence in an issue. This document does not promise a staffed or continuously monitored reporting channel.
